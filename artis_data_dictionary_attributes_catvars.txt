reference_hs6_taxa_resolution	match_category	broad_taxa_match	"Applied to production taxa reported at a coarse taxonomic level (order or above: e.g., Osteichthyes, Clupeiformes, Bivalvia) that cannot be resolved to a specific hs6 via the standard matching logic. These taxa are matched broadly to all hs6s whose descriptions reference the relevant taxonomic group (e.g., Clupeiformes _ any hs6 mentioning herring, anchovy, or sardine). Also applied via broad_matches to any higher-level taxon (genus through kingdom) found in prod_taxa_classification that was not already matched directly. Assigned in: match_hs_to_taxa() (addendum block at the end of the function)"
reference_hs6_taxa_resolution	match_category	explicit_taxa_match	"The hs6 was matched to a taxon by directly identifying the taxon's scientific name (species, genus, family, order, or class) in the hs6 description text, or via common name where scientific classification lacks sufficient resolution (e.g., shrimps/crabs via match_by_common_name()). For FMFO codes, species flagged as primary fishmeal species (primary_fishmeal == 1) are also upgraded to this category. This is the most specific and reliable match type.
